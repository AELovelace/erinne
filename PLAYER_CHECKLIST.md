# Multiplayer casino play checklist

1. Open Media → Games and choose Blackjack, Roulette, Horse Racing or Slot Machines.
2. Create a table. On another account or a signed-out paired TV, open the same game and join its table code. For several remotes sharing one TV, use the shared-TV instructions below instead.
3. Use **Left/Right** to change your pick and **Up/Down** to change the stake by ten (10–500, within your balance). **Enter sets the bet** on each device; selecting a pick or stake alone does not submit it.
4. The host presses **Play** (the remote's Play/Pause button, or **P** on a keyboard) to start, or everyone waits for the betting timer. In blackjack, Left/Right chooses **Hit** or **Stand**, and Enter confirms before the hand timer expires.
5. Slots show three reels and three horizontal paylines (top, middle, bottom), with separate reels for each player. The selected bet is **per line**: 50 costs **150 total**. All three lines play; paying rows light up and show their individual returns. Columns and diagonals do not pay. Check the shared round results on both screens. New betting opens after ten seconds.
6. Roulette shows all 37 pockets, a spinning rotor and counter-rotating ball. The ball lands in the server's winning pocket. Refresh to resume your seat; touch and mouse buttons work too. In the lobby, arrows still choose a table and Enter joins. Keyboard Tab reaches individual buttons; Escape/Back returns to Games.
7. Choose **Leave table** to remove your seat; its active wager is forfeited and your other coins stay yours. Returning to Games alone keeps your seat available. Tables expire after two idle hours and hand back any bets still open.

Online tables use your account's coins, shared by every game, and a TV uses the coins of the account it's paired to. Coins have no cash value. If your balance is below the minimum wager (30 for three-line slots, ten for other games), use **Refill free coins** during betting. A new mid-round player watches until the next round. In ordinary Create table mode, a remote panel controls its TV's seat; separate accounts or paired TVs provide separate seats.

## Several casino players on one TV

1. Pair the TV and each remote under the **same owner's account** in Media → Screens. Give remotes distinct names, such as Pink remote and Blue remote. On each remote, select that TV.
2. Open Blackjack, Roulette, Horse Racing or Slot Machines on the paired TV and select **One TV, multiple remotes**. Each remote presses **Enter once to join**. The first player is host, and all eight seats are available to players.
3. Find your remote's name and player number on the TV. Left/Right changes only your pick; Up/Down changes only your stake. **Enter again places your bet**. Your selected draft and your submitted bet are shown separately. The host remote's **Play/Pause** starts the round; the timer also starts it.
4. In blackjack, each remote independently selects Hit/Stand with Left/Right and confirms with Enter. Slots show every player's reels and line returns. A remote joining mid-round waits for the next betting window.
5. Refreshing resumes the same seats, balances and selections. A fresh tab can choose **Resume on this TV** in the lobby. These tables play for free credits, 1,000 per player at each table, and never touch anyone's coins. If credits fall below the minimum, Enter during betting refills them. The remote's **Stop** closes the game for everyone on that TV; Back to Games keeps the table saved.
6. A keyboard, mouse or touchscreen on the TV can **Remove player** or **End TV table**; removed balances and active wagers are forfeited. Removing the host passes hosting to the first remaining player. Remote arrow/Enter inputs cannot accidentally activate these management buttons. Removing/re-pairing a remote invalidates its old controls; remove any abandoned seat on the TV.

Ordinary **Create table** keeps the original one-seat-per-account/TV behavior. The shared-TV mode is explicit, and other devices can still join its code. Remotes paired to a different owner cannot control this TV. Check the actual physical remotes before release.

## Lumi Pet

1. Open **Media → Games → Lumi Pet** on a browser or paired TV. Left/Right selects Cuddle, Snack, Play or Nap/Wake gently; Enter interacts. Touch/click activates an activity directly. Tab reaches individual buttons.
2. Up changes the glow between Rose, Lagoon and Starlight; Down toggles sound. Escape/Back returns to Games when embedded.
3. Try each activity and check the response, happy-moment count and last memory. Lumi sleeps at night (22:00–07:00 on this device); a daytime nap lasts two minutes. Interacting gently wakes Lumi for fifteen minutes.
4. Refresh to keep memories and colour. They belong to this browser profile, shared by its users, and do not sync to other devices. Clearing browser storage clears them. If saving is blocked, the page explains that this visit cannot be saved.
5. There are no hunger meters, streaks, chores or penalties for being away. Check narrow-screen layout, reduced motion and real TV controls before release.

## TV sizing

Games follow **Screens → Text size**, including while a game is already open. **Automatic** keeps the existing 1080p size, uses 1.5× at 2560×1440 and 2× at 3840×2160 (CSS viewport sizes). A saved manual size takes precedence; choose Automatic to restore resolution-based sizing. Bigger/Smaller and Cancel update the game without restarting the round or pet visit. Check readable stakes/cards on the 4K TV, all eight shared-TV player cards, and unchanged sizing on the 1080p TV. Game frames should have no duplicate TV toolbar or second screen margin.
