# Multiplayer casino play checklist

1. Open Media → Games and choose Blackjack, Roulette, Horse Racing or Slot Machines.
2. Create a table. On another account or a signed-out paired TV, open the same game and join its table code.
3. Choose a 10–500-chip stake and, for roulette or horses, a pick. Select **Place bet** on each device.
4. The host selects **Start round**, or everyone waits for the betting timer. In blackjack, select **Hit** or **Stand** before the hand timer expires.
5. Check the same round result on both screens. Slots have separate reels for each player. New betting opens after ten seconds.
6. Refresh to verify your seat resumes. Use arrows and Enter on a TV remote; touch and mouse work too.
7. Choose **Leave table** to remove your seat; its chips and active wager are forfeited. Returning to Games alone keeps your seat available. Tables expire after two idle hours.

Chips are free, per table and have no cash value. If your balance is below ten, use **Refill free chips** during betting. A new mid-round player watches until the next round. A remote panel controls its TV's seat; separate accounts or paired TVs provide separate seats.
