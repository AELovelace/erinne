Access

Shared libraries

Libraries are shared folders of documents. People with read access can browse them and get answers from them; editors can also upload, organise and delete files. Private inboxes are never shared. Claude lets someone search a library from Claude Desktop, which sends the passages found to Anthropic: it needs the library's switch on and Claude ticked on their grant.

Groups

Grant a library to a group and everyone in it gets that access.

Who can see what

Each person's inbox and libraries, the access they have, whether they can use it from Claude, and the grants that give it.
