# Erynne on a phone

Everything works on a phone: Ask, Files, the Wiki, Rooms, Media and the rest. The pages rearrange themselves for a narrow screen, and touch takes the place of the mouse.

## Installing it as an app

Open Erynne in Chrome on Android and choose **⋮ → Install app** (or **Add to Home screen → Install**). It then opens like an app, without the browser's address bar, and appears in the **Share** menu of other apps such as YouTube.

- Press and hold its icon for shortcuts to **Media**, **Screens** and **Files**.
- On an iPhone, **Share → Add to Home Screen** in Safari installs it too. iPhones don't let web apps appear in the Share menu, so copy the link and paste it in **Media → Screens** instead.

## Finding your way around

- The tabs sit in a strip under the top bar; swipe it sideways to reach the rest.
- **☰** at the top right holds your name, **Help** and **Sign out**.
- **✉** is Messages, as on a desktop.

## Ask

- **☰ Chats** above the thread opens your earlier questions; **＋ New** starts a fresh one.
- **Enter** makes a new line; press **Ask** to send.
- **Advanced settings** opens the tools panel over the page; tap outside it to close it.

## Files

- Tap a folder to open it, and a file to view it. The **📁** button by the path opens the list of places; the path itself takes you back up.
- **Press and hold** a file or folder for its menu (rename, move, copy, delete, properties and so on), as you would right-click with a mouse.
- **Select** turns taps into ticks, for several files at once; ticking a box does the same. **Actions ▾** then offers what can be done with them. Press **Done** to stop selecting.
- **ⓘ** shows a file's details and preview over the list; **✕** closes them.
- Uploading a folder at once needs a computer; files upload from the **Upload** menu as usual.

## Rooms, Wiki and the rest

- In Rooms, **☰** opens the list of channels and people; tap a message to see its actions (react, reply in a thread, pin). **Enter** makes a new line; **Send** sends.
- In the Wiki and the Help, the page list folds above the page; tap **Pages ▾** or **Contents ▾** to open it.
- In Media, the menu is a row above the shelves. When a TV is chosen under **Play on**, the remote sits at the bottom of the screen. See [Media](media.md#playing-on-the-tv-from-your-phone).
- The Markdown editor shows **Source** or **Preview**, one at a time; **Save** stays at the top.
