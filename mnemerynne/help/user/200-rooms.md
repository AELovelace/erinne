# Rooms

**Rooms** is the team's chat inside Erynne: channels for a team or a topic, and direct messages with one person or a few. Everything arrives as it's written, with no need to reload. The badge on the Rooms tab counts messages that mention you and unread direct messages.

## Channels and direct messages

- **Channels** have a name like `#front-office`. They come in three kinds:
  - **open** (`#`): anyone can read them and join them;
  - **private** (🔒): only the people invited;
  - **tied to a group** (👥): whoever is in that directory group, automatically, so joining or leaving the group adds or removes you.
- **Browse channels** lists the open ones and your group ones; press **Join** or **Open**. Writing in an open channel joins it.
- **＋** starts a direct message (type a name, or several names separated by commas for a group of up to nine) or makes a channel.
- A **dot** beside a person shows whether they're here now (green), away for a while (yellow) or not (grey).

## Writing

- **Enter** sends; **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. Markdown works: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `` `code` ``, lists, quotes and links.
- **@name** mentions someone. **@channel** reaches everyone in the channel, and **@here** only those who are here now. Mentions light up and count on the badge.
- **📎** (or dragging files onto the messages) attaches files, up to 25 MB each. Pictures show in the room; other files download. **Save to my files** copies one into your File box.
- A link to a file or wiki page in Erynne shows a card with its name, but only for people who can open it. Everyone else sees a plain link.

## On a message

Point at a message for its buttons:
- **😊** react;
- **💬** reply in a thread (the conversation opens on the right, and the message shows how many replies it has);
- **📌** pin it (the channel's **Pins** lists them);
- **✏️** edit it (yours only; it shows *(edited)*);
- **🗑** delete it (yours, or anything in a channel you own; it shows *message deleted*).

## Asking Erynne

Write **@Erynne** and a question, and she answers in a thread under it, citing her sources. Because everyone in the room reads her answer, she only uses documents **every member of the room** can read. In an open channel that means what everyone in the organisation can read, so she may know less there than when you ask her privately on **Ask**. She can only read here; to have her change files or draft email, use Ask. A channel's owner can turn her off under **Settings**.

## Notifications

**Settings** in a room sets what counts: every message, only mentions, or nothing. It also offers desktop notifications, which show direct messages and mentions while Erynne's tab is in the background.

## Through Claude

With Claude connected, Claude can read your rooms (`list_rooms`, `read_room`), write in them (`post_to_room`, shown as "Claude for you") and react, if its connection may edit. Everyone in the room sees what it posts.

The **Inbox** entry at the top of the sidebar is not a room: it holds short notes and what Erynne tells you about your account and proposals ([Messages and announcements](messages-and-announcements.md)); the envelope button at the top right opens it. Room messages are kept for a year.
