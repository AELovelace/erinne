# Welcome to Erynne

Erynne is your organisation's private document assistant. She answers questions from the documents you are allowed to see, cites the exact page, and keeps your files in one place. Everything runs on your organisation's own server: no document, question or answer leaves it unless you deliberately connect Claude (see [Claude](claude.md)).

The product behind her is called **Mnemerynne**. You will see that name on the sign-in page, in a few admin screens and in this help; "Erynne" is the assistant you talk to.

## The tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Ask** | Ask questions in plain language and get cited answers. See [Asking questions](ask.md). |
| **Files** | Your own File box and the libraries shared with you: upload, organise, preview, restore earlier versions. See [The Files tab](files.md). |
| **Wiki** | Your organisation's wiki: pages anyone with access can read, and people with write access can edit in the browser. See [The organisation wiki](wiki.md). |
| **Remote** | Web pages on the organisation's own network, opened through Erynne in a small browser window. Only shown when an admin listed one for you. See [The Remote tab](remote.md). |
| **✉ (top right)** | Notes from colleagues, what Erynne tells you (a proposal decided, a comment on your file) and announcements; with Rooms on, the Inbox in Rooms. See [Messages and announcements](messages-and-announcements.md). |
| **Preferences** | Your account, theme, password, signed-in browsers, memories, Microsoft 365 and Claude. See [Preferences](preferences.md). |
| **Access** | Only admins see this: libraries, people, groups and server settings. |

Top right you will find **Help** (this wiki, opens in a new tab) and **Sign out**.

## What Erynne can and cannot do

- She answers **only from documents and internal systems you can access**. If the answer is not in them she says so instead of guessing.
- Every answer carries **citations** that open the source at the right page, heading, slide or rows. Trust the citation, not the summary, when it matters.
- She remembers the **context of a conversation** (follow-up questions work) and, depending on your server's settings, a few **facts about you** that you can see and delete at any time ([Memories](memories.md)).
- Depending on your groups she may also have **tools**: searching live systems such as the ticketing system, reading your mail and calendar, and drafting changes to files. Nothing she drafts happens until you approve it ([Tools and proposals](tools-and-proposals.md)).
