# Asking questions

The **Ask** tab is a chat with Erynne. Type a question the way you would ask a colleague ("How do I run the night audit?", "What's the pet policy at Portland?") and press **Ask** or Enter.

## What you get back

An answer streams in as it is written, followed by **Sources**: the documents it came from. Every claim in the answer carries a small numbered link; click it to open the document at that exact place (see [Scopes and citations](scopes-and-citations.md)).

If the documents you can see don't answer the question, Erynne says so plainly rather than making something up. That is deliberate: she only answers from your documents and the internal systems you may use, and an answer without a source is never shown as fact.

## Follow-up questions

Within a conversation, Erynne remembers what was asked before, so "and at the Portland property?" or "what about weekends?" works. Each conversation is listed on the left; **New question** starts a fresh one. Click a conversation to reopen it; its answers and sources are kept. The ✕ next to a conversation deletes it (Shift-click skips the confirmation).

Questions are limited to 2,000 characters.

## Searching in one place

By default Erynne searches **all your documents**: your File box and every library shared with you. The 🔎 chip under the question box opens a picker to search only one library or one folder for the questions that follow. The chip shows where you are searching; each answer also shows the scope it used. Choose **All my documents** to go back.

## Feedback

Under each answer, 👍 and 👎 record whether it helped, with an optional comment. Admins use this to find documents that are missing or out of date and to tune Erynne; it never changes your access.

## Advanced settings (people with Erynne's tools)

If your account is in the group that gets Erynne's tools, an **⚙ Advanced settings** box appears. It lists every tool she may use for your questions (searching, reading, browsing, live systems, mail, drafting files) with a switch for each; switching one off applies from your next question, and **Switch them all back on** resets them. Switching tools *on* never gives you access you don't have. See [Tools and proposals](tools-and-proposals.md).

A small counter on the right shows how much text the models read and wrote for this conversation.

## Use External API

If your admin has turned this on for you and you have added your own API key on Preferences, a **🌐 Use External API** switch appears. With it ticked, the question is answered by Claude (Anthropic's model) using your key and billed to you, with Erynne's tools, but reaching only the documents that are shared with Claude; your mail, calendar and memories never go there. Changes it drafts are still proposals you apply. Without a key the switch is replaced by a link to add one.

## Good questions

- Use the words your documents use. Erynne knows some hotel jargon ("night audit" and "End of Day" are the same thing), but the closer you are to the document's wording, the better the hit.
- One question at a time. Two unrelated questions in one message get one muddled answer.
- If the first answer misses, narrow the scope to the right library or ask with the system's own term.
