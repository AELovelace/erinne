# Memories

Erynne can keep short facts about you between conversations, so she can tailor answers: which property you work at, your role, your shift. They are shown on [Preferences](preferences.md#what-erynne-remembers-about-you) under *What Erynne remembers about you*.

## Three modes

Your admin chooses one for the whole server; the heading on Preferences tells you which applies:

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Automatic** | When you mention a fact about yourself in a question ("I work at the Portland property"), Erynne keeps it. |
| **Explicit** | She only remembers what you ask her to: "Remember that I work at the Portland property." |
| **Off** | Nothing is remembered between conversations. |

## What is never saved

A filter runs before anything is stored. Passwords, codes, card numbers, guest names and guest details are never kept, even if you say them. There is also a cap on how many memories one person has (50 by default); older ones make room for new ones.

## Who can see them

Only you. Admins can wipe a person's memories (for example when someone leaves) but cannot read them. Memories are never sent to Claude.

## Forgetting

- **Forget** next to a memory removes that one.
- **Forget everything** removes them all.

Both take effect at once; your next question no longer uses them.

## Conversation context is different

Within one conversation Erynne also remembers what you asked before, so "and in Portland?" after "what time is checkout?" works. That context belongs to the conversation, not to you, and goes when you delete the conversation on the Ask tab.
