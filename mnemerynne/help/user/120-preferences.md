# Preferences

Preferences is your own page: the left half is about you and Erynne, the right half is about using Erynne from Claude. Nothing here affects other people.

## Your account

The top shows your username, your **role** (user, local admin, or admin through a server setting or a domain group), your **domain account** if an admin linked one, the **groups** you are in and when the account was created. Groups come from the domain and from Mnemerynne's own groups; admins use them to grant libraries, tools and internal systems. If something you expected is missing (a library, Erynne's tools), this is the first place to look: the group that grants it is probably not listed.

## Theme

Two themes, applied the moment you click:

- **Sakura**: blossoms over Mt Fuji, following your device's light or dark mode.
- **Neon**: hot pink on black, always dark.

The choice is saved to your account and remembered by this browser for the sign-in page.

## Password

Enter your current password and the new one twice. Passwords must be at least 8 characters. After the change every other browser is signed out; this one stays signed in, and Claude connections keep working. Wrong current passwords count towards the sign-in lockout (see [Signing in](sign-in.md#wrong-passwords-and-lockouts)).

## Signed-in browsers

**Sign out other browsers** ends every session except this one. Use it when you left yourself signed in on a shared computer.

## What Erynne remembers about you

The facts Erynne keeps to tailor your answers, each with a **Forget** button, and **Forget everything**. See [Memories](memories.md) for what is stored and when.

## Microsoft 365

Appears when your organisation has set it up. Link your work account so Erynne can include SharePoint in your answers and, for people with her tools, read your mail and calendar and draft replies. See [Microsoft 365](microsoft-365.md).

## Claude

The right half: using your own Anthropic API key in chat (when your admin has turned that on for you), adding Erynne as a connector in Claude on the web, phone and Desktop, what Claude can search and change for you, and your personal tokens. See [Claude](claude.md).
